    Pick your platform

    Your exact plugin and script line are on your results page after a scan. The line looks like this:

    <script src="https://your-site-bridge/widget.js" data-site="your-site-id" defer></script>

    Prefer not to touch code? Our team installs it, connects it to your content and checks it on your live site.

    Contact our team for setup
    WordPress
    1. On your results page, click Download for WordPress.
    2. In WordPress, go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the .zip, then Install and Activate.
    3. Done. An "Ask AI" button appears on every page. Change its colour or turn it off in Settings → Site Bridge Agent.
    Shopify
    1. Go to Online Store → Themes, open the theme menu and choose Edit code.
    2. Open layout/theme.liquid and paste your script line just above </body>.
    3. Save. The agent appears on every page of your store.
    Webflow
    1. Open Site settings → Custom code.
    2. Paste your script line into Footer code and save.
    3. Publish your site.
    Wix
    1. Go to Settings → Custom code → Add custom code.
    2. Paste your script line, choose All pages and place it in Body – end.
    3. Apply. Custom code needs a Wix premium plan with a connected domain.
    Squarespace
    1. Go to Settings → Advanced → Code Injection.
    2. Paste your script line into Footer and save.
    Any other website
    1. Paste your script line just before </body> in your main template (HTML, React, Next.js, Laravel and more).
    2. Using Google Tag Manager? Add it as a Custom HTML tag that fires on All Pages.
    AI assistants and agent pipelines (MCP)
    1. Copy your MCP address from the Agent pipeline tab on your results page.
    2. In Claude Code run claude mcp add --transport http your-site your-mcp-address. Cursor, VS Code and Claude Desktop configs are on the same tab.
    Your own chatbot, RAG or help centre
    1. Open the Knowledge base tab on your results page.
    2. Download it as Markdown or JSON and load it into your chatbot, custom GPT, RAG pipeline or help desk.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common questions about Site Bridge.

    What does "agentic website" mean?

    Your site gains an AI agent that understands all of its content and can operate the interface: open pages, highlight things, fill forms. AI tools can also connect to it through an MCP server.

    How do I add the agent to my website?

    On WordPress, upload the plugin from your results page and activate it. On Shopify, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace or any other site, paste one script tag before the closing body tag. The install guide shows where for each platform, or our team can set it up for you.

    Can I try it before setting it up?

    Yes. Scan your site and open the live demo: your real website with the agent inside it. The demo lets you ask two suggested questions so you can see it answer and move around your pages. For the full agent, contact our team for setup.

    Do I need to change my website's code?

    No. Scanning and the live demo need nothing from you. Going live is one plugin on WordPress, or a single script tag anywhere else.

    Will the agent use my brand colours?

    Yes. It detects your primary button and link colour and your font from the page itself. You can also set a colour explicitly on the script tag.

    Can the agent submit forms or make purchases?

    It fills fields with details the visitor gives, then asks for confirmation before any submit, purchase or payment. In the demo, forms are never actually sent.

    What is the MCP server for?

    It lets AI assistants like Claude, Cursor and others look up your site: its profile, a search across every page and the full text of any page, so your business shows up correctly inside AI tools.

    Can I use the knowledge base in my own chatbot?

    Yes. Download it as Markdown or JSON from your results page. It holds your site profile and the full text of every page with its source address, ready for a chatbot, custom GPT, RAG pipeline or help centre.

    Which websites work best?

    Public marketing, product, service and e-commerce sites. Pages behind logins aren't crawled, and very JavaScript-heavy sites need the optional headless browser.
